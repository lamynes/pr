About PELICAN REEF

Pelican Reef in Theodore, Alabama is a laid-back seafood spot with a marina view that locals love and visitors remember. Nestled along Fowl River at 11799 Dauphin Island Parkway, it’s just a short drive from Mobile or Dauphin Island — and it feels like a hidden gem once you arrive.

Scenic waterfront dining: You can sit inside or out and enjoy peaceful views of the river and boats drifting by.

Hours: Open Wednesday to Sunday, typically 11 AM to 9 PM (closes at 8 PM on Sundays).

Phone: (251) 973-2670

Atmosphere: Family-friendly, pet-welcoming on the deck, and known for generous portions and friendly service.

If you’re ever cruising down Dauphin Island Parkway and craving seafood with a view, Pelican Reef is worth the stop. Want help planning a visit or checking out the full menu? I’ve got you covered.
